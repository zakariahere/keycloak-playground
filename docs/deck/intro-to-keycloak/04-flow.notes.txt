The key point: the browser only ever carries a single-use code. Tokens are exchanged over a direct server-to-server call. Even if someone captures the code from the URL, it is useless without the client secret and the PKCE verifier.

PKCE exists because public clients (SPAs, mobile) cannot keep a secret at all.
