Reading the status

200Token valid, authority present.
401 UnauthorizedNo token, expired token, bad signature, wrong issuer or wrong audience. The WWW-Authenticate header above names the exact reason.
403 ForbiddenToken perfectly valid — the identity just lacks the required role. 401 means “I don't know you”; 403 means “I know you, and no”.
