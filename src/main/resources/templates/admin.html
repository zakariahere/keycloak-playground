Gated by .requestMatchers("/admin/**").hasRole("app-admin").

You reached this page, so your ID token carried app-admin in realm_access.roles and the authorities mapper turned it into ROLE_app-admin.

Experiment

Open the Keycloak console, remove app-admin from bob, then reload this page. You still get in — because your ID token was minted before the change and this app trusts it until it expires.

That lag is inherent to token-based auth, not a Keycloak bug. Shorten token lifetimes to shrink the window, or introspect on every request and give up the offline-validation speed.
