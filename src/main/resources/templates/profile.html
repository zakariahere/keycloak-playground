Granted authorities

These are what hasRole() and hasAuthority() actually test.

ROLE_app-user

The SCOPE_* entries come from Spring's defaults. The ROLE_* entries only exist because userAuthoritiesMapper() reads realm_access.roles out of the ID token. Delete that bean and every role check in the app silently starts failing.
